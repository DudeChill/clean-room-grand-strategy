# Release review v0.1.0

Milestone: **0.4 Land Warfare / 0.5 Logistics** — the foundation release.

## What ships

A playable, deterministic, headless-capable grand-strategy simulation with an
original 2,275-province world and a ten-country 1936-style scenario:

* **Simulation**: fixed hourly tick, twelve ordered phases, seeded RNG streams,
  command pipeline with validation/logging/replay, per-subsystem hashing.
* **Land warfare**: division templates whose statistics emerge from battalion
  composition; incremental organisation/strength combat with soft/hard attack,
  defence, breakthrough, armour, piercing, hardness, combat width, reinforcement,
  terrain, weather, experience, planning, entrenchment, supply; physical retreats and
  destruction when surrounded; fronts, offensive and fallback lines.
* **Industry**: resources, construction with per-project capacity allocation,
  production lines with efficiency growth and switch retention, equipment
  stockpiles, consumer goods, synthetic refineries, fuel.
* **Research**: prerequisite graph, research slots, year-based cost, unlock
  propagation into equipment and modifiers.
* **Logistics**: supply sources, graph propagation with capacity and falloff,
  emergent encirclement, per-division supply and fuel, supply debugger.
* **Politics and diplomacy**: political power, laws, stability, war support,
  manpower; relations, factions with joining and succession, war coalitions,
  capitulation, territorial transfer, peace, occupation with resistance.
* **AI**: five layers acting exclusively through the command API, with scored
  reasons recorded for every decision.
* **Persistence**: versioned sectioned saves with per-section hash verification,
  command log, replay metadata.
* **Client**: browser app with a canvas map (political/control/supply/terrain/front
  overlays), production/construction/research/military/diplomacy/log panels, battle
  and supply detail, alerts, time controls, keyboard shortcuts, in-game save/load.

## Known gaps (tracked, not hidden)

* **BLOCKER**: air warfare (AIR-001), naval warfare (NAV-001), focus trees (POL-001).
* **MAJOR**: events/decisions, national spirits, trade and convoys, equipment
  designers, intelligence, multiplayer transport.
* **MODERATE**: battle plan execution without micromanagement, combat tactics,
  strategic redeployment, ports and overseas supply, motorised distribution, AI air
  and naval layers, mod load order.
* **MINOR/COSMETIC**: UI scale and accessibility options, selection groups, named save
  slots, map/data editors.
* **Tuning**: the AI is aggressive in an all-AI observer game — one year of the
  shipped scenario produces 31 wars and three capitulations. The thresholds are
  constants in `ai.cpp` (`kDipWarThreshold`, the 1.5 target force ratio, the 30-day
  declaration cooldown) and are recorded in `docs/mechanics/ai_scoring.md`; balancing
  them is deliberate follow-up work, not a hidden change (TODO AI-009).

Every entry lives in `docs/DISCREPANCIES.md` with severity, reference behaviour,
current behaviour and status; `docs/PARITY_MATRIX.md` records the status of every
feature with the evidence behind it.

## Reproducing this build

```sh
cmake -S . -B build -DCMAKE_BUILD_TYPE=Release && cmake --build build -j
build/hoi_tests
scripts/verify.sh          # the full release gate used for this review
build/game --serve --port 8080 --player VEL
```
