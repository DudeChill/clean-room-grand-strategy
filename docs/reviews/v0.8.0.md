# Release review v0.8.0

Milestone: **equipment variants and continuous replacement** (MIL-021, MAJOR) — the link
that turns research and production into fielded capability.

## What is new

* **A slot names a family, not a model.** `slot_family` maps a battalion slot to its
  equipment family; `equipment_fits_slot` admits any model of that family the country may
  field, refusing locked models and other countries' designs; `preferred_slot_model`
  decides which member a country issues — the one on a live production line first (a
  country fields what it builds, and the choice cannot oscillate), then the best stocked
  member, then the best fieldable member.
* **Continuous replacement**: `phase_reinforcement` runs after production and before the
  AI. A division below full strength draws `max(missing × 0.02, 0.25) × supply` per hour
  of the model the country issues for each of its slot families; it takes what the
  preferred model has and makes up the remainder from whatever else of that family is in
  the depot, so a division never starves next to usable gear. Supply gates it, so a
  cut-off or encircled division does not refill. No new state: the save format and the
  world hash layout are unchanged.
* **Training draws the same way** — family-grouped, with the same depot fallback — so new
  divisions and existing ones agree on what the army is equipped with, and a country
  retooling to a new model does not stall its training queue.
* **AI and demand agree with the draw**: the AI's template mask, equipment readiness and
  production need are family-aware, so a design is recognised as "what my divisions are
  made of" instead of being invisible next to its archetype.
* **Client**: a selected division now lists the models it actually fields, so an upgrade is
  visible rather than inferred.
* **Scenario integrity**: the generator now derives the technology gate of every starting
  model (lines, depots, wings, task forces), grants what is missing and fails loudly if a
  gate cannot be satisfied — closing the case of two countries starting with a live line
  for equipment they were not allowed to field.

## Known gaps (tracked, not hidden)

* **ECON-004 (MINOR)**: the AI piles up gear the army cannot use — 346,738 units of one
  design in the depot at day 365 while 2,989 are in the field; demand does not net off
  stock already held.
* **AI-014 (MINOR)**: the industry layer assigns factories against pre-loss control counts
  and self-heals a tick later, logging a warning each time.
* Remaining MAJORs: **INT-001** intelligence, **MP-001** multiplayer transport, **MOD-002/003**
  mod load order and validation tooling.
