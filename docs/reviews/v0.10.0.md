# Release review v0.10.0

Milestone: **intelligence** (INT-001, the last MAJOR simulation subsystem) — agencies, spy
networks, operations, decryption, and two honest effects on battle.

## What is new

* **Agency**: 13 upgrades bought with political power and gated by year, technology, trigger
  and prerequisites; each axis is real (network growth, operation speed, crypto speed,
  counter-intelligence) and they are chained, so investing in one delays the others.
* **Networks and operations**: 10 operations covering all six kinds. Networks grow at
  `0.40/day` (halved against a country you are not at war with, cut by the target's
  counter-intelligence), operations run at a pace set by upgrades and by how well the
  network covers the requirement, and completion applies the data's effects — research
  days, a timed modifier on the target, or a script effect — before rolling the burn
  chance on the intel RNG stream.
* **Decryption**: `0.15/day` at base, faster with upgrades, slower against a competent
  target; knowledge is capped at 0.85, so a player always has something left to guess.
* **Battle effects, in exactly two places**: the attacker's planning bonus is scaled by
  `(1 - 0.50 × decrypted)` and attack power by `(1 + 0.10 × intel_level)`. Both are zero
  when the attacker knows nothing — a battle between two blind countries is byte-identical
  to the engine without this subsystem.
* **AI**: buys the cheapest eligible upgrade, keeps networks in the countries it worries
  about, and picks operations by value (political power never enters the score). It keeps
  one operation in flight so the agency is not drained.
* **Client**: an Intelligence tab — agency upgrades with reasons for what is disabled,
  per-target network strength, exposure, decryption and intel level, in-flight operations
  with a progress bar and cancel, and startable operations per target.

## Known gaps (tracked, not hidden)

* `ideology_shift` is carried as a **war-support** modifier: the engine has no ruling-party
  support vector, so "support ideology" cuts the target's war support. Recorded as a known
  difference, not a silent approximation.
* `counter_intel_level` reads agency upgrades only — there is no counter-intelligence
  modifier kind in the national modifier stack yet.
* **MP-001** multiplayer transport remains the last MAJOR; **MOD-004** scenario modding,
  **ECON-004** and **AI-014** are MINORs.
* Full list in `docs/DISCREPANCIES.md`.
