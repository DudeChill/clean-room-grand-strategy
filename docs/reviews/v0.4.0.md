# Release review v0.4.0

Milestone: **0.7 Politics and Diplomacy** — national focus trees (POL-001, the last
BLOCKER), events, decisions and a data-driven script engine.

## What is new since v0.3.1

* **Script engine**: one vocabulary of triggers, effects and scopes shared by focuses,
  events and decisions — comparisons, boolean combinators, randomness on the events RNG
  stream, country/state/variable scopes, and effects from political power through
  technologies, laws, claims, wars, scripted modifiers and chained events.
* **National focus trees**: data-defined focuses with prerequisites, mutual exclusions,
  availability and bypass triggers, durations and effect blocks; a country selects one
  at a time and completes it over days; the AI scores available focuses.
* **Events**: automatic and chained firing with delays, `fire_only_once`, immediate and
  per-option effects, and AI option choice by weight.
* **Decisions**: visibility, availability, political-power cost, active timers, removal
  effects and cooldowns, with AI evaluation.
* **Scripted modifiers**: timed or permanent modifier blocks granted by content feed the
  same country modifier stack as technology and laws.
* **Client**: a Politics tab — focus tree with completion state and prerequisites, the
  active focus with its progress, pending events with their options, and the decision
  list with costs and timers.
* **Content**: shared industry/army/politics trees plus country trees, a set of events
  and decisions, all fictional and generic.
