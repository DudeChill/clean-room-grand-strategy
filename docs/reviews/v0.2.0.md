# Release review v0.2.0

Milestone: **0.6 Air Warfare** (added on top of the v0.1.0 foundation).

## What is new since v0.1.0

* **Air warfare (AIR-001, previously the top BLOCKER)**
  * Air wings are entities: aircraft model, planes versus establishment, base
    province, mission region, mission, efficiency, experience, cumulative losses.
  * Missions: air superiority, interception, close air support, strategic bombing,
    logistics strike, reconnaissance.
  * Air combat between hostile wings in the same region, with documented loss and
    experience model; losses become replacement demand drawn from the equipment
    stockpile.
  * Air control per strategic region is derived from the wings actually flying there
    and feeds land combat as an additive modifier (`BattleDebugLine::air_mod`), so
    the combat debugger shows the whole chain including the air term.
  * Close air support damages enemy divisions in the region's battles; strategic
    bombing reduces the target state's factories; logistics strike cuts railway
    levels (and therefore supply capacity).
  * Wings whose base is lost are displaced to the nearest friendly air base with free
    capacity, or destroyed when none exists.
* **Anti-air is a real building** (AIR-002 closed): `Province::anti_air` reduces
  bombing and logistics-strike damage and raises attacker losses over defended ground.
* **Aircraft content**: fighter/cas/tactical bomber archetypes with 1936 and 1940
  models driven by air attack, air defence, ground attack, agility and range; an air
  technology tree that unlocks them; air bases guaranteed at every capital and in
  every large industrial state; the two largest powers start with a fighter and a CAS
  wing.
* **Client**: an Air tab (form wings from stockpiled aircraft, change missions,
  disband, see air-control per contested region) and an air-control map overlay; the
  battle detail now shows the air term of the damage decomposition.
* **22 new balance constants** for the air model now live in data
  (`data/common/constants.json`), not in code.

### Air behaviour in a live campaign

With wings formed at scenario start and the AI flying them, a one-year observer run
shows: wings drawing replacements from production, air control shifting between
regions as the front moves, CAS damage landing in the battle debug lines
(`air_mod`), bombing reducing the factories of frontline states, and wings displaced
(or destroyed) when their air bases are overrun. Air costs 0.005 ms per simulated
hour, so the whole air system is cheaper than the supply network it feeds.

## Known gaps (tracked, not hidden)

* **BLOCKER**: naval warfare (NAV-001), focus trees (POL-001).
* **Air model gaps**: no detection roll (AIR-003), no fuel or pilot accounting for
  sorties (AIR-004), reconnaissance flies but reveals nothing because there is no
  fog of war (AIR-005), and air never attacks ships or ports until the naval
  milestone provides targets.
* **MAJOR**: events/decisions, national spirits, trade and convoys, equipment
  designers, intelligence, multiplayer transport.
* Everything else is recorded in `docs/DISCREPANCIES.md` with severity and status.
