# Release review v0.7.0

Milestone: **equipment designers** (ECON-002, MAJOR) and the **resource economy
rebalance** (ECON-003). A country now designs, builds and fields its own equipment
models, and the map no longer hands out free resources.

## What is new

* **Designers**: fit components (armour, weapon, engine, airframe, hull, special) into an
  equipment archetype. The engine computes the resulting statistics, cost and resource
  use, registers the variant as a real `EquipmentDef`, and records it as the country's
  design. `src/sim/design.cpp`, formula documented in `ARCHITECTURE.md` §5.11.
* **Component content**: 64 components across armour (16), artillery (8), infantry (8),
  aircraft (16) and ships (16); 45 are year-gated, 4 are technology-gated, 2 carry
  `available` triggers. Every authored field is read by the loader (field-drop bug fixed:
  `defense`/`breakthrough` were silently dropped).
* **Designs are upgrades**: a sensible 1936 fit beats the matching 1936 model —
  armour +29.5% capability for +7.5% cost, artillery +22.8% at -27.5% cost,
  infantry +15.8% at -48.8% cost.
* **AI**: `ai_design_layer` designs for the categories a country produces, accepting a fit
  when capability gain >= 10% within a +25% cost margin, acting only through commands.
  Templates that name a bare archetype now redirect their demand to the best model of that
  family the country can actually build, and tech-locked models are no longer targeted.
* **Ownership**: a design belongs to its author; `equipment_unlocked` refuses to let
  another country research, build or field it (found by measurement: countries were
  building each other's designs).
* **Client**: a designer section in the Production panel — pick an archetype, fit one
  component per slot (locked parts are shown disabled), name the variant, create it, and
  put it into production.
* **Resources**: province yields cut hard (worldwide source provinces: steel 553 -> 325,
  aluminium 200 -> 244 (regated), rubber 39 -> 51, chromium 98 -> 33, tungsten 99 -> 27),
  capital-ship per-factory-day resource costs cut ~10x (battleship 180 steel -> 9), and
  scenario industry now scales with owned states. Consequence: **9 of 10 countries run at
  least one genuine deficit** and 12 trade routes (10 active) close them, where the old map
  produced surpluses everywhere and three routes.

## Known gaps (tracked, not hidden)

* **Division slots accept only their exact equipment id**: a newly designed model reaches
  the field through new units only, not by retrofitting existing divisions. Recorded as a
  new MAJOR discrepancy with the measured evidence.
* Components cover five categories; support, anti-tank, anti-air, motorized, mechanized and
  convoy archetypes have none yet.
* **MAJOR**: intelligence, multiplayer transport.
* Full list in `docs/DISCREPANCIES.md`.
