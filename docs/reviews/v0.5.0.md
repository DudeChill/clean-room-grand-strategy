# Release review v0.5.0

Milestone: politics completed — **national spirits and political advisors** (POL-006).

## What is new since v0.4.0

* **National spirits**: permanent, named, data-defined modifier blocks with availability
  triggers and slot costs. They feed the same country modifier stack as technology and
  laws, can be granted by content (focuses, events, decisions) through the script
  effect `add_national_spirit`, and are visible in the client with their source.
* **Political advisors**: appointments bought with political power, occupying advisor
  slots and granting modifiers until dismissed. The script engine can grant them too.
* **AI**: the politics layer now appoints advisors and adopts spirits, scored against the
  country's posture (war favours military modifiers, peace favours industry and research),
  with the same recorded reasons as every other layer.
* **Client**: the Politics tab lists the spirits a country holds (with free slots) and the
  advisors in office, plus the choices available to adopt or appoint.
* **Content**: a set of fictional spirits and advisors, some trigger-gated, wired into the
  1936 scenario for the largest powers.
